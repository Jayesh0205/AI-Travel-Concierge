GOA TRAVEL GUIDE

Overview:
Goa is a popular tourist destination in India known for its beaches, Portuguese-influenced heritage, nightlife, food, and relaxed atmosphere.

North Goa:
North Goa is known for popular beaches, nightlife, markets, restaurants, and water activities.

Popular places in North Goa:
- Baga Beach
- Calangute Beach
- Anjuna Beach
- Vagator Beach
- Fort Aguada

South Goa:
South Goa is generally known for quieter beaches, scenic surroundings, and a more relaxed travel experience.

Popular places in South Goa:
- Palolem Beach
- Colva Beach
- Benaulim Beach
- Agonda Beach

Panaji and Old Goa:
Panaji is the capital of Goa and is useful as a base for exploring central Goa. Old Goa is known for historic churches and heritage sites.

Popular attractions:
- Basilica of Bom Jesus
- Se Cathedral
- Fontainhas
- Dona Paula

Travel Planning:
A 3-day Goa trip can be divided into North Goa, central Goa and Old Goa, and South Goa.

Example 3-day structure:
Day 1:
Explore North Goa beaches and nearby attractions.

Day 2:
Visit Panaji and Old Goa and explore heritage locations.

Day 3:
Explore South Goa beaches and enjoy a relaxed day.

General Tips:
- Plan travel time between different parts of Goa.
- Keep important documents and personal belongings safe.
- Check local conditions and attraction timings before visiting.
- Choose accommodation based on the areas you plan to visit.
