GENERAL TRAVEL PLANNING GUIDE

Trip Planning:
A good travel plan should consider destination, trip duration, budget, transportation, accommodation, activities, and available time.

Itinerary:
An itinerary should organize activities by day and should avoid scheduling too many activities in a short period.

Transportation:
Travelers should consider the distance between attractions and the time required for transportation when planning each day.

Accommodation:
Accommodation should be selected according to budget, location, safety, and proximity to planned activities.

Budget:
A travel budget can include transportation, accommodation, food, sightseeing, local travel, and emergency expenses.

Safety:
Travelers should keep important documents secure, stay aware of their surroundings, follow local rules, and check current conditions before traveling.

Personalization:
A useful travel recommendation should consider the traveler's destination, duration, interests, budget, preferred activities, and transportation preferences.
